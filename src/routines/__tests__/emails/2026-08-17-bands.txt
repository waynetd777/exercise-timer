WARM-UP – ±8 MINUTES
40 seconds each – continuous movement
1. Easy Jog → High Knees
2. Jumping Jacks
3. Hamstring Curls + Arm Pulls
4. Lateral Lunges + Overhead Reach
5. Knee Drives + Opposite Elbow
6. Fast Feet → 2-Step Shuffle
Mobility – 30 seconds each
* World’s Greatest Stretch – alternate sides
* Inchworm + Shoulder Tap
* Arm Swings – across chest + overhead
* Standing Hip Rotations + Torso Twist

#1 GENERAL BODY
3–6–9–12–15–12–9–6–3
Complete the full count of one exercise before moving to the next.
* Thrusters – squat + press
* Alternating Reverse Lunges + Knee Drive
* Plank Shoulder Taps – each side
* Burpees – step-back option for low impact
* Sit-up + Reach

Bonus: After completing the ladder, 30 seconds fast mountain climbers

#2 ARMS & SHOULDERS
4 Rounds
* 12 × Bicep Curls
* 10 × Arnold Press
* 12 × Bent-Over Reverse Flyes
* 15 × Band Pull-Aparts
* 10 × Upright Rows
* 20 × Alternating Cross Punches
No rest between exercises.
Rest 45 seconds after each round.

#3 LEGS
20–16–12–8–4–8–12–16–20
Complete the main exercise at each count.
Main Exercise:
Goblet Squats
After every Goblet Squat set:
* 10 × RB (resistance band) Lateral Walks – 5 each direction
* 10 × RB Glute Kickbacks – 5 each leg
Band Burner after the ladder:
* 15 × RB Squats
* 15 × RB Side Steps each direction
* 20 × Glute Bridge + RB Abduction
* 20-second RB Squat Hold

#4 CORE
3-5 Rounds
* 12 × Heel Taps – each side
* 10 × Plank Knee-to-Elbow – each side
* 12 × Toe Touches
* 10 × Alternating Leg Raises – each leg
* 30-second Forearm Plank

#5 LEGS FINISHER – PYRAMID BURN
4–8–12–16–20–16–12–8–4
Main Exercise:
Alternating Curtsy Lunges
After every set:
* 10 × Squat Jumps
or squat + calf raise for low impact
* 15 × Calf Raises
* 10-second Wall Sit

FINAL BURNOUT – NO STOPPING
Complete once:
* 20 × Sumo Squats
* 20 × Alternating Reverse Lunges
* 20 × Squat Pulses
* 30-second Wall Sit
