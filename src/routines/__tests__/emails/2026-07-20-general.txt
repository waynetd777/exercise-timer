Warm-up
40 sec each (continuous movement):

1. March → Jog on the spot
2. Jumping Jacks (floor or trampoline)
3. Butt Kicks
4. Side-to-Side Squats with a Reach (start standing, step out to one side, sink your hips into a squat, and reach your arms across your body for an added stretch)
5. Standing Knee Hugs (alternate legs)
6. Toy Soldier Kicks (straight-leg kicks with opposite hand reach)

Then finish with 30 sec each:

* World’s Greatest Stretch (alternate sides)
* Inchworms (walk hands out to a plank and back)
* Arm Circles (15 sec forward, 15 sec backward)
* Torso Rotations with arms extended

#1 General Body
Counting: 2-4-6-8-10-8-6-4-2

Complete one exercise before moving to the next.

* Squat + Shoulder Press
* Speed Skaters (each side)
* Push-ups (knees or toes)
* Mountain climbers
* V-Ups

#2 Arms & Shoulders
4 Rounds:
* 12 × Hammer Curls
* 10 × Shoulder Press
* 8 × Lateral Raises
* 6 × Bent-over Rows
* 20 × Front Punches

No rest between exercises. Rest 45 seconds after each round.

#3 Legs
15-12-9-6-3-6-9-12-15

* Sumo Squats

After every set:

* 10 × Walking Lunges (5 each leg)
* 10 × Glute Kickbacks (each leg) with resistance band

#4 Core
5 Rounds
* 12 × Bicycle Crunches
* 10 × Dead Bugs (each side)
* 12 × Russian Twists (each side)
* 10 × Reverse Crunches
* 30-second Plank

🔥 After Round 5:

* 20 Flutter Kicks
* 20-second Hollow Hold

#5 Legs Finisher – Burnout Ladder

Counting: 5–10–15–20–15–10–5

Perform the following:

* Squat Pulses

After every set:

* 10 × Alternating Jump Lunges (or Reverse Lunges for low impact)
* 15 × Calf Raises
* 10-second Wall Sit

🔥 Final Burnout

Complete without stopping:

* 20 × Sumo Squat Pulses
* 20 × Alternating Curtsy Lunges (10 each leg)
* 30-second Wall Sit
* 30 Squat Pulses
