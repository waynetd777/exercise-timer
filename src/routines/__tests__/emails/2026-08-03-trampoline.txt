Trampoline Warm-up (5 Minutes)
30 seconds each (continuous movement)
1. Easy Bounce (basic).
2. Jog on the Spot – Increase the tempo.
3. Jumping Jacks
4. Knee lifts
5. Butt Kicks
6. Ski Jumps – Side-to-side jumps with feet together.
7. Jump-jump squat
Sprint Finish – Fast feet for 15 seconds

#1 General Body
Counting: 3-6-9-12-15-12-9-6-3
Complete one exercise before moving to the next.
* Squat to Knee Lift
* Burpees (step-back option if needed)
* Alternating Curtsy Lunges
* V-Ups (or Crunch Reach)

#2 Arms & Shoulders
5 Rounds
* 12 × Hammer Curls
* 10 × Shoulder Press
* 12 × Lateral Raises
* 10 × Bent-over Rows
* 20 × Front Punches + 20 × Uppercuts
Rest: 30–45 seconds after each round.

#3 Legs
Counting: 20-16-12-8-4-8-12-16-20
Main Exercise:
* Sumo Squats
After every set:
* 10 × Reverse Lunges (each leg)
* 15 × Resistance Band Walks (forward & back)
* 15 × Glute Bridge Marches

#4 Core
4 Rounds
* 20 × Dead Bugs (10 each side)
* 15 × Russian Twists (each side)
* 12 × Reverse Crunches
* 20 × Plank Hip Dips (10 each side)
* 30-second Hollow Hold (legs up 45 degrees if your back takes strain)
After Round 4
* 20 × Flutter Kicks (each leg)
* 20 × Cross-Body Mountain Climbers
* 45-second High Plank

#5 Legs Finisher
Counting: 8-12-16-20-16-12-8
Main Exercise:
* Bulgarian Split Squats (alternate legs each set, or perform half the reps per leg)
After every set:
* 15 × Fire Hydrants (each leg)
* 15 × Standing Calf Raises
