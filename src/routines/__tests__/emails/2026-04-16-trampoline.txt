Warmup

10 x forward/backward run
3 x quads stretch
8 x forward/backward run
3 x hamstring stretch
6 x forward/backward run
3 x quads & hamstring stretch

1#

5 x Reverse lunge + knee drive (left & right) (Tab 1)

10 x Sumo squats (Use weights)

5 x Hand-release push-ups (left & right stagger optional) (Can go quite fast) (Tab 2)

10 x Plank toe taps (Plank position - hand to opposite toe – go back to plank position before doing next rep) (Tab 3)

5-10-15-20-15-10-5 Mountain climbers (Use trampoline)


2#

10 reps – 10 mins (Use timer on phone)

10 x Dead bug with reach (Arm back - opposite leg down - SLOW) (Tab 4)
10 x Leg raises with hip lift (Tab 5)
10 x Plank up-downs (elbows to hands) (Tab 6)

3#

Finisher (Till time is up) 5mins (Use timer on phone)

1-2-3-4-5-6-… (keep climbing)

Exercises:
• Jump squats (Arms out to the front - alongside the ears) (Tab 7)
• Alternating step-back lunges
• Shoulder taps push-ups (or normal push-ups if needed) (Tab 8)

4#

Optional Burner (if you want to burn some extra)

AMRAP 3–5 mins:
• 5 x Burpees (no push-up) (Tab 9)
• 10 x Bicycle crunches
• 15 x Bodyweight squats
