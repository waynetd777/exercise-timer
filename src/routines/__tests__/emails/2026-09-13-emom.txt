#1 GENERAL BODY
10-MINUTE AMRAP
As many quality rounds as possible in 10 minutes
* 12 × Squat + Shoulder Press
* 10 × Alternating Reverse Lunges – 5 each leg
* 12 × Plank Shoulder Taps – 6 each side
* 8 × Squat thrusts
* 16 × Russian Twists – 8 each side
* 10 × Mountain Climbers – each side

#2 ARMS & SHOULDERS
HARD EMOM – 6 MINUTES
Work continuously — aim to finish each minute with only 5–10 sec rest.

Minute 1
15 × Bicep Curls
* 10 × Push-Ups

Minute 2
12 × Arnold Press
* 10 × Cross Punches each side

Minute 3
15 × Bent-Over Rows
* 10 × Plank Shoulder Taps

Minute 4
20 × Band Pull-Aparts
* 10 × Lateral Raises

Minute 5
12 × Shoulder Press
* 12 × Front Raises

Minute 6
10 × Squat thrust
* 20 × Punches

Repeat × 2

Rule: If you finish early, NO full rest — keep moving with light punches or marching until the next minute.

FINISHER – 2 MINUTES
30 sec each:
1. Punches + Squat
2. Push-Up + Shoulder Tap
3. Punches + High Knees
4. ALL OUT BURPEES

#3 LEGS
30/15 INTERVAL
4 rounds
30 sec WORK / 15 sec TRANSITION

1. Goblet Squats
2. RB Lateral Walk + Squat
3. Alternating Reverse Lunge + Knee Drive
4. RB Glute Kickbacks – switch halfway
5. Glute Bridge + RB Abduction
6. Squat Pulses

➡️ NO 30-second rest between rounds.

After each round:
10 Squat Jumps

FINAL ROUND

Replace squat pulses with:
30 sec Squat Hold + 10 pulses at the end

#4 CORE
COUNTING CHALLENGE
5–10–15–20–15–10–5
Complete:
* Heel Taps – each side
* Bicycle Crunches – each side
* Alternating Leg Raises – each leg
* Russian Twists – each side

No rest between exercises.

After completing the ladder:
3 ROUNDS
30 sec each
* Forearm Plank
* Plank Knee Drives
* Side Plank – Right
* Side Plank – Left

10 sec transition only.

CORE FINISHER
60 seconds nonstop

20 sec Mountain Climbers
20 sec Bicycle Crunches
20 sec Mountain Climbers

#5 FULL-LEG BURN – LADDER CHALLENGE
3 ROUNDS
Complete the reps without resting between exercises.

Round 1
* 10 × Sumo Squats
* 10 × Alternating Curtsy Lunges
* 15 × Calf Raises
* 10 × RB Squats
* 10 × Squat Pulses

Round 2
* 15 × Sumo Squats
* 14 × Alternating Curtsy Lunges
* 20 × Calf Raises
* 15 × RB Squats
* 15 × Squat Pulses

Round 3
* 20 × Sumo Squats
* 18 × Alternating Curtsy Lunges
* 25 × Calf Raises
* 20 × RB Squats
* 20 × Squat Pulses

AFTER EACH ROUND

30 seconds nonstop:
* Squat Jumps
OR

Only 15 seconds rest between rounds.

FINAL LEG BURN – 2 MINUTES

30 sec Wall Sit
30 sec Squat Pulses
30 sec Wall Sit + Heel Raises
