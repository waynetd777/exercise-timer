🔥 Warm-up

* Cardio: 20–30–45–30–20 sec (any movement)
* 10 Bodyweight squats
* 10 Alternating reverse lunges
* Quad stretch × 3 (left & right)
* Hamstring stretch × 3 (left & right)
* Glute stretch × 3 (left & right)

#1 General Body – Around the Clock (10 minutes)(PHONE TIMER)

Complete 5 rounds. Work at your own pace with as little rest as possible.

* 20 × High knees (Jogging trampoline)
* 16 × Squat + Front Raise
* 12 × Alternating Reverse Lunges (6 per leg)
* 8 × Renegade Rows (or plank shoulder taps if no weights)
* 4 × Burpees (or squat thrusts for a low-impact option)

💥 Bonus Challenge
After every round, perform 20 Mountain Climbers (10 per side) before starting the next round.

#2 Arms & Shoulders

5 Rounds

* 10 × Upright Rows
* 10 × Arnold Presses
* 10 × Bent-over Reverse Flyers
* 20 × Fast Cross Punches (10 each arm) (Trampoline)

Rest as needed.

#3 Legs

10–8–6–4–2–4–6–8–10

* Goblet Squats

After every set:

* 8 × Curtsy Lunges (each leg)

Use heavy dumbbells

#4 Core – 4 Rounds (Tabata timer)

* 10 × Sit-up & Press (Trampoline)
* 20 × Heel Taps (10 each side) (Trampoline)
* 10 × Toe Touch Crunches (Trampoline)
* 20 sec Plank
* Round 1: Forearms
* Round 2: Right Side
* Round 3: Forearms
* Round 4: Left Side

#5 Leg Finisher

2-4-6-8-10-8-6-4-2
* Squat jumps (or regular squats for a lower-impact option) (Trampoline)

After every round:

* 10 × Calf Raises
* 10 × Squat Pulses

🔥 Challenge: Finish with a 60-second wall sit to empty the tank! 💪🏻 ‎
