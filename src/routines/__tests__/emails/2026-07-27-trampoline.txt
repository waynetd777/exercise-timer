Warm-up (Tabata timer)



40 sec each (continuous movement):

1. Jogging (Trampoline)
2. Jumping Jacks (Trampoline)
3. Butt Kicks (Trampoline)

4. Knee lifts (Trampoline)
4. Side-to-Side Squats with a Reach (Start standing, step out to one side, sink your hips into a squat, and reach your arms across your body for an added stretch)
5. Toy Soldier Kicks (Straight-leg kicks with opposite hand reach)

Then finish with 30 sec each:

* World’s Greatest Stretch (Alternate sides) (Arm up on the side of the forward knee)
* Inchworms (Walk hands out to a plank and back) (Bend over put one hand down first then walk them forward until you are on tippy toes)
* Arm Circles (15 sec forward, 15 sec backward)
* Torso Rotations with arms extended (Arms forward and twist)


#1 General Body



Counting: 3-6-9-12-9-6-3



Complete one exercise before moving to the next



* Squat + Press

* Plank Jacks

* Alternating Reverse Lunges

* Sit-up with Punch (Sit up, double punch and then down)



#2 Arms & Shoulders



3 Rounds



* 10 × Arnold Press

* 12 × Upright Rows

* 10 × Bicep Curl to Press

* 6 - 12 × Rear Delt Flies

* 20 × Speed Punches



Rest 30–45 seconds after each round.



#3 Legs



Counting: 12-8-4-8-12-(16) (Complete all reps of the main exercise)



Main Exercise:



* Alternating Reverse Lunges



After every set:



* 12 × Glute Bridges

* 15 × Resistance Band Side Steps (each direction)



#4 Core



3 Rounds:



* 15 × Toe Touch Crunches

* 12 × Heel Taps (each side)

* 15 × Plank Shoulder Taps (each side)

* 12 × Leg Raises (hands underneath lower back)

* 30-second Side Plank (15 sec each side) (TABATA timer)



After Round 3:



* 30 Mountain Climbers

* 20 Bicycle Crunches

* 30-second Forearm Plank (TIMER-set to 35 seconds)



#5 Legs Finisher



Counting: 8-12-16-12-8



Main Exercise:



* Goblet Squats (or Bodyweight Squats)



After every set:



* 8 × Single-leg Romanian Deadlifts (each leg)

* 12 × Squat Pulses

* 10 × Calf Raise Pulses



Final Burnout (No Rest)



* 20 Jump Squats (or Bodyweight Squats)

* 20 Alternating Lateral Lunges (With weights-start with weight in front of each foot and alternate picking them up and putting them down)

* 20 Glute Bridge Pulses (With weights)

* 45-second Wall Sit

* 30-second Squat Hold (Stand on weights and hold weights)
