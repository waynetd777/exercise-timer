#1 GENERAL BODY


10-MINUTE AMRAP (As Many Rounds As Possible)
As many quality rounds as possible in 10 minutes:


* 10 × Squat + Shoulder Press
* 8 × Bulgarian split squat – 4 each leg
* 10 × Plank Shoulder Taps – 5 each side
* 6 × Burpees
* 12 × Russian Twists – 6 each side
Every time you finish a round:
10 Mountain Climbers

#2 ARMS & SHOULDERS – EMOM


5-Minute EMOM (Every Minute On the Minute)

Repeat 2 rounds

Start a new exercise every minute.
Work → rest for the remainder of the minute.

Minute 1: 12 × Bicep Curls
Minute 2: 10 × Arnold Press
Minute 3: 12 × Bent-Over Rows
Minute 4: 15 × Band Pull-Aparts
Minute 5: 12 × Lateral Raises + 10 Cross Punches


🔥 Finisher:
30 seconds Punches + Squat
